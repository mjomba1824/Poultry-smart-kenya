About Our Business

Poultry Smart Kenya is an educational and marketing platform designed to support poultry farmers, beginners and customers in Kenya.

Our mission is to share practical poultry farming knowledge, connect buyers with poultry products, and encourage better farm management and agribusiness planning.

Our Objectives

  • Educate farmers on poultry farming.
  • Promote broiler and kienyeji chicken farming.
  • Help farmers market their products.
  • Encourage proper farm record keeping.
  • Promote responsible and sustainable agribusiness.

Poultry Farming Education

Learn essential skills for starting and managing a poultry farm.

🐣 Broiler Farming

Learn how to raise chickens for meat, manage housing, provide appropriate feed, monitor growth and prepare birds for market.

Topics Covered

  • Choosing healthy chicks
  • Housing and ventilation
  • Feeding and clean water
  • Hygiene and biosecurity
  • Growth and cost records

🐔 Kienyeji Farming

Discover how to manage indigenous chickens for meat, eggs and breeding.

Topics Covered

  • Housing and free-range management
  • Feeding and nutrition
  • Egg collection
  • Chick care
  • Marketing chickens and eggs

💰 Poultry Agribusiness

Learn how to plan your poultry business and manage its finances.

  • Startup budgeting
  • Calculating expenses
  • Finding customers
  • Keeping sales records
  • Estimating profit and loss
